§ 02   What this is

NSH Studio is where I keep the things I make away from a keyboard — tote bags I sew, sketches I draw, objects I print.

It moves slowly on purpose. Most things take a few weekends. Some never finish.

If something here catches your eye, say hello. I sometimes make extras.
